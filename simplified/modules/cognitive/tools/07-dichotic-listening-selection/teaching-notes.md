# Teaching notes - Dichotic Listening and Selection Theories (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/cognitive/tools/07-dichotic-listening-selection/`

**What it teaches.** A physical change to a stream students are told to
ignore, a sudden switch to capital letters, is often noticed even though
they cannot say what the ignored words actually meant.

**Before students start.** Tell students only to read the left column
and ignore the right, nothing more. A skip route exists for anyone who
would rather not perform the task.

**What students do.**
1. Watch word pairs and press whenever the attended, left-hand word is
   a colour word.
2. Say whether anything about the ignored, right-hand column changed
   partway through.
3. If yes, guess which category the ignored words belonged to.

**What to look for.** Most students notice the capital-letters switch on
the ignored side even while focused on the colour-word task, but far
fewer can correctly name the ignored category, which is only a
one-in-four guess at best.

**Debrief.** If you noticed the switch but could not name the category,
what had actually been processed about the ignored column? Why use a
physical change, capital letters, rather than asking students to notice
a change in meaning?

**Common misconception / caution.** "This is dichotic listening" needs
correcting: it is a visual analogue, and reading, unlike listening, is
voluntary. This is one trial for one student, so noticing or missing the
switch says nothing about that student's attention in general.

**Use in class / timing.** This one is quick: 4 to 6 minutes.
