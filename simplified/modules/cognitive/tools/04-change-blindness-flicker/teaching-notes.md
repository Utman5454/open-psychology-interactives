# Teaching notes - Change Blindness (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/cognitive/tools/04-change-blindness-flicker/`

**What it teaches.** A blank screen inserted between two otherwise
identical scenes removes the local flash or motion a change would
normally produce, turning instant pop-out into slow, effortful search.

**Before students start.** The second scene is deliberately different
from the first, so it cannot be solved from having already seen the
answer.

**What students do.**
1. Search for the change with the blank in place and say what changed.
2. Check the reveal.
3. Repeat on a different scene, this time with the blank switched off.
4. Compare how long the two searches took.

**What to look for.** The blank version usually takes much longer than
the no-blank version, even though the second scene is a change nobody in
the room has seen before.

**Debrief.** Why use a different scene the second time rather than the
same one again? What does the blank actually remove?

**Common misconception / caution.** Do not let "so people don't store
anything between glances" stand unchallenged. The paradigm compares
across views; it says nothing about storage. Two scenes and one student
prove nothing about that person, only about what removing the blank does
to detection.

**Use in class / timing.** Both scenes together take about 5 to 7
minutes.
