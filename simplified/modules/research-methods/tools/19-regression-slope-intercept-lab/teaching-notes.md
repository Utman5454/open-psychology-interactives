# Teaching notes - Regression: Intercept, Slope and Least Squares (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/research-methods/tools/19-regression-slope-intercept-lab/`

**What it teaches.** Least squares finds the one line that makes the
total area of the squared residuals as small as possible, which is
where the method's name comes from.

**Before students start.** None needed. The squares are already drawn
when the page opens.

**What students do.**
1. Move the intercept and slope sliders and watch the drawn squares
   and their total area change.
2. Press "show me the best line" and compare it with your own attempt.

**What to look for.** The total squared area keeps shrinking as the
line gets closer to the best fit. A deliberately bad starting line can
give a total area larger than simply predicting the average for
everyone.

**Debrief.** Why does the method use squared distances rather than
just the distances themselves?

**Common misconception / caution.** The plain residuals from a
least-squares line always sum to zero; it is the sum of their squares
that the method actually minimises, not the sum of the residuals
themselves.

**Use in class / timing.** Fitting a line by hand and checking it
takes about 5 to 7 minutes.
