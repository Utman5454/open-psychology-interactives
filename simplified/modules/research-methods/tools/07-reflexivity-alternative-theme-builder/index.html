Two questions, two lenses, four accounts

Nothing here analyses anything. Four accounts were written in advance, one per pairing. Selecting a pairing selects a prepared account, because no software analyses qualitative data and pretending otherwise would teach the opposite of the point.

Key terms
Theoretical lens
The set of assumptions an analyst reads with: what counts as an explanation, and what kind of object the account is about.
Reflexivity
Treating the analyst's position as part of the analysis rather than as contamination to be removed from it.
Coherence
Whether the question and the lens are asking after the same kind of object. A mismatch is visible from outside and does not depend on judging the analyst's skill.
Central, supporting, at the margins
How much weight an extract carries in a given account. The same extract can be the whole argument under one reading and a passing detail under another.

        What this shows

        Several readings, not any reading

        Two of these four pairings are strong and two pull against themselves. The difference is not the analyst's skill and it is not a matter of taste: it is whether the question and the lens are asking after the same kind of object, and a reader can see it from outside.

        Key idea: The account a qualitative analysis produces is not sitting in the transcript waiting to be found. It follows from what was asked and from the position it was read from, and changing either changes which extracts are central, which recede, and what the analysis can conclude. That is not a licence for anything: an account that answers a question nobody asked is visibly incoherent, which is what makes reflexivity a standard rather than an excuse.

        Nothing here analysed anything. Four accounts were written in advance and selecting a pairing selects one of them. Two questions and two lenses is a teaching simplification: there are many more of both, and a narrative, psychosocial or feminist reading would produce accounts again different from these. The extracts are invented and no participant, interview, institution or study is represented.
