# Teaching notes - Reflexivity and Alternative Theme Builder (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/research-methods/tools/07-reflexivity-alternative-theme-builder/`

**What it teaches.** A research question and a theoretical lens
jointly determine which extracts become central. In this activity's
four accounts, the two coherent pairings and the two strained ones
sit on opposite diagonals of the same grid.

**Before students start.** Changing either control never adds or
removes anything from the six extracts, only what the account is
entitled to say.

**What students do.**
1. Choose a research question and a lens from the two options each.
2. Read the resulting account, then change one control and compare.
3. Find at least one strong pairing and one strained one.

**What to look for.** Changing either control on its own can move a
pairing from coherent to strained.

**Debrief.** Nothing in the six extracts changed between your strong
and strained pairings; what did?

**Common misconception / caution.** "So it is all interpretation,
anything goes" is worth catching here too: half the four pairings are
rated strained, which is external evidence that not every combination
is equally defensible.

**Use in class / timing.** Trying a few question-lens pairings takes
about 5 to 6 minutes.
