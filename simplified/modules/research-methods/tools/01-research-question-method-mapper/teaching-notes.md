# Teaching notes - Research Question to Method Mapper (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/research-methods/tools/01-research-question-method-mapper/`

**What it teaches.** A research question's wording implies an aim, and
that aim is what a method has to answer, before any design or test
gets chosen.

**Before students start.** This version stops after aim and kind. If
you want to take it further, ask verbally what design each question
would imply.

**What students do.**
1. Read the belonging question, choose its aim, then its kind.
2. Do the same for the mindfulness question.
3. Do the same for the group-work question.

**What to look for.** Watch for students defaulting to "quantitative"
whenever a question mentions a number, rather than reading what the
question is actually asking for.

**Debrief.** Two of the three questions could be answered by more than
one design. Which, and why does the question's wording not settle it
alone?

**Common misconception / caution.** "You look at the data and pick the
test" is worth heading off here too: nothing in this activity ever
reaches a test, because it stops at aim and kind.

**Use in class / timing.** All three questions together take about 5
to 6 minutes.
