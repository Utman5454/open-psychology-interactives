# Teaching notes - ANOVA F-Ratio Visualiser (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/research-methods/tools/10-anova-f-ratio-visualiser/`

**What it teaches.** F is the ratio of between-groups to within-groups
variance, and separating the means, shrinking the spread within
groups, or adding participants can each raise it on its own.

**Before students start.** None needed. The simulation is already
running when the page opens.

**What students do.**
1. Draw a fresh sample and adjust the separation between group means,
   the within-group spread, or the number of participants.
2. Watch F, the p-value and the share of variance explained move as
   each slider changes.

**What to look for.** Pushing up the sample size alone can turn a
small, unremarkable gap into a significant result.

**Debrief.** Which of the three sliders moved F the most, and does
that tell you anything about which change would matter most in a real
study?

**Common misconception / caution.** "A big F means a big effect" is
worth catching here too: a large sample size alone can produce a
sizeable, significant F for a difference nobody would call important.

**Use in class / timing.** Drawing a few samples and reading the
readout takes about 5 to 7 minutes.
