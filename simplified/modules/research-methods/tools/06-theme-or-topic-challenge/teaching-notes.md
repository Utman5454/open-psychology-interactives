# Teaching notes - Theme or Topic? (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/research-methods/tools/06-theme-or-topic-challenge/`

**What it teaches.** A theme is an analytic claim organised around one
idea, not a summary of what is most common, and a true,
reasonable-sounding descriptive sentence is not automatically a theme.

**Before students start.** None needed. Each candidate is judged
before the next is shown, so the sort cannot be solved by elimination.

**What students do.**
1. Classify four candidate statements, one at a time, before seeing
   the next.
2. Read the tally and the notes on each.

**What to look for.** The descriptive, reasonable-sounding candidate
is the one most often mistaken for the theme itself.

**Debrief.** What made the descriptive candidate feel like a theme
even though it made no analytic claim?

**Common misconception / caution.** "A theme is what most people
said" is worth catching here too: prevalence is not the test being
applied in this sort.

**Use in class / timing.** Sorting the four candidates takes about 4
to 5 minutes.
