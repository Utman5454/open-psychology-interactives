# Teaching notes - Sampling Bias Simulator (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/research-methods/tools/04-sampling-bias-simulator/`

**What it teaches.** Convenience sampling drifts systematically off
the true mean no matter how many students are recruited, while simple
random sampling centres on it.

**Before students start.** This is a generated population of fictional
students, not a real survey.

**What students do.**
1. Choose a recruitment method and a sample size.
2. Run one study, then run two hundred, and watch where the pile of
   estimates centres.

**What to look for.** Convenience sampling's pile centres well off the
true mean even at a large sample size. Simple random sampling's pile
centres on it from the start, and only gets narrower at larger sample
sizes.

**Debrief.** Two methods both use 600 people. Why does only one of the
piles centre on the true value?

**Common misconception / caution.** "A bigger convenience sample gets
you closer to the truth" is the one to catch: the pile narrows around
the wrong place. More people recruited the same biased way is more
precision about the wrong number.

**Use in class / timing.** Running a method or two takes about 5 to 6
minutes.
