# Teaching notes - Where the Line Goes (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/personality-individual-differences/tools/55-personality-disorder-continuum/`

**What it teaches.** Four fictional profiles are fixed; only which
rule draws the line, counting unusual traits or counting how much
difficulty a person's traits cause, and where the line sits, changes
who falls above it. At a threshold of 70 on the interference rule, two
of the four profiles sit above the line; switching to the traits rule
at the same threshold swaps which two.

**Before students start.** None needed.

**What students do.**
1. Predict whether the same four profiles would be sorted the same way
   by a rule based on unusual traits and a rule based on difficulty
   caused.
2. Read which two of the four profiles sit above the line at the
   default threshold on the interference rule.
3. Switch to the traits rule at the same threshold and see which two
   profiles swap sides.
4. Move the threshold from its lowest to its highest setting and watch
   the share of a fictional population above the line fall sharply.

**What to look for.** "Unusual traits, managing well" and "ordinary
traits, a great deal of difficulty" swap sides between the two rules,
while the other two profiles stay on the same side under either one.
Moving the threshold from 55 to 90 on the interference rule drops the
population share above the line from about 27% to under 1%, with none
of the four fixed profiles changing at all.

**Debrief.** Two profiles swapped sides when the rule changed. What
does that tell you about what each rule is actually counting?

**Common misconception / caution.** A profile moving above or below a
line when the threshold or the rule changes is not evidence that the
underlying difficulty is arbitrary or unreal; the categories are a
convention drawn across genuine variation, and unusual traits alone
are not the same thing as the difficulty they can, but do not always,
cause.

**Use in class / timing.** Comparing the two rules at one threshold
and then sweeping the threshold takes about 5 to 7 minutes.
