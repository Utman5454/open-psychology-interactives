# Teaching notes - Same Average, Six Very Different Weeks (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/personality-individual-differences/tools/50-self-esteem-stability-tracker/`

**What it teaches.** Three fictional people share exactly the same
six-week average, 62, yet move through the six weeks completely
differently. Ada and Cleo swing by about the same amount day to day,
close to 3 points, while Bo swings by about 7.5; delivering the
identical work setback to all three drops Cleo by 22 points and Ada by
only 5.5, and Ada is back within three days while Cleo has still not
recovered by the end of the six weeks.

**Before students start.** Ada, Bo and Cleo's day-to-day movement is generated automatically
when the page loads; nothing needs to be prepared.

**What students do.**
1. Predict whether an identical average over six weeks means three
   people are equally stable.
2. Compare the three people's day-to-day movement before any event is
   delivered.
3. Deliver the same work setback to all three and compare the size of
   each person's drop.
4. Compare how many days each person takes to return close to their
   average afterward.

**What to look for.** The identical setback produces very different
drops, largest for Cleo, because her reaction to that particular
domain is much stronger than Ada's or Bo's. Ada recovers within a few
days; Cleo has still not returned to her average by the end of the six
weeks, even though her day-to-day wobble on an ordinary week is
smaller than Bo's.

**Debrief.** All three people average 62 over six weeks. Is that
enough to say any of them is more stable than the others?

**Common misconception / caution.** An identical average level says
nothing about volatility, reactivity to a particular domain, or how
quickly someone recovers from a setback; these are separate
characteristics, and this activity delivers the same event to
everybody precisely so any difference in outcome comes from the
person, not from what happened to them.

**Use in class / timing.** Comparing the three people's wobble and
their response to one delivered event takes about 5 minutes.
