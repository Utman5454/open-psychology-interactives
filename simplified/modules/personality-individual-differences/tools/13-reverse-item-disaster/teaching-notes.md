# Teaching notes - Reverse-Item Disaster (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/personality-individual-differences/tools/13-reverse-item-disaster/`

**What it teaches.** Two of six items are reverse-worded and nobody
has recoded them yet. As scored, alpha is -.04 and four of the six
items correlate negatively with the total, including two items that
were never reverse-worded at all. Recoding one reverse item at a time
shows the second one getting worse before both are fixed: alpha moves
from -.04 to +.03 to +.78 as the two repairs are made in sequence.

**Before students start.** The six items and six fictional respondents are fixed from the
start; only which scoring method is applied changes.

**What students do.**
1. Predict what leaving two reverse items unrecoded does to the
   scale's reliability.
2. Inspect the as-scored item statistics and notice which items
   correlate negatively with the total.
3. Recode one reverse item at a time and watch alpha and the
   remaining item's correlation change.
4. Compare the broken ranking of six respondents against the
   correctly scored one.

**What to look for.** Recoding only the first reverse item does not
fix the scale: the second reverse item's correlation with the total
gets worse, not better, because the total it is being compared against
is still broken. Only once both are recoded does alpha reach .78 and
every item correlate positively with the total. The six respondents'
ranking under the unrecoded scoring bears no resemblance to their
correct ranking.

**Debrief.** Recoding the first item made the second item's statistics
look worse. Does that mean the first recoding was a mistake?

**Common misconception / caution.** A negative item-total correlation
does not always mean an item is reverse-worded and forgotten: here two
of the four negative items were never reverse items at all, they only
look broken because the total they are being measured against is
itself broken. Fixing one problem in a scale can temporarily make an
unrelated number look worse before the whole thing is repaired.

**Use in class / timing.** Working through both recodings and
comparing rankings takes about 6 to 7 minutes.
