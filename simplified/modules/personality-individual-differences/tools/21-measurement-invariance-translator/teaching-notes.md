# Teaching notes - The Same Difference, Twice Over (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/personality-individual-differences/tools/21-measurement-invariance-translator/`

**What it teaches.** Group B always scores 0.20 higher than Group A on
the observed total, whatever the blend slider is set to. At one end,
that whole difference sits in a single item's intercept and the two
groups are identical on the trait; at the other end, group B genuinely
stands higher and the difference spreads across all four items in
proportion to their loadings. The total cannot tell these two
situations apart; only the pattern across items can.

**Before students start.** Groups A and B carry no nationality,
ethnicity or culture; the mechanism is about a setting an item is
answered from, not about who answers it.

**What students do.**
1. Predict whether a fixed observed difference between two groups can
   come from more than one underlying cause.
2. Move the blend slider from one end to the other and watch the same
   0.20 total redistribute across the four item bars.
3. Read off the two extremes: one item carrying the whole difference,
   or all four items sharing it in proportion to their loadings.

**What to look for.** At one end of the slider, only one item's bar
shows a difference and the other three sit at zero; the two groups'
latent means are identical. At the other end, all four bars show a
difference sized to that item's loading, and group B's latent mean is
genuinely higher than group A's. The four bars always sum to the same
total, whichever end the slider sits at.

**Debrief.** Two settings of the slider produce the same total group
difference. What single piece of evidence, visible on this page, tells
them apart?

**Common misconception / caution.** A fixed total difference between
two groups is compatible with a real difference in the trait and with
no difference at all; the total alone cannot distinguish them, and the
same is true of any real total score compared across two groups.

**Use in class / timing.** Moving the slider between its two ends and
comparing the bars takes about 5 minutes.
