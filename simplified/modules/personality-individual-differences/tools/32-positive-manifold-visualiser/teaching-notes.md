# Teaching notes - A General Factor With No General Ability (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/personality-individual-differences/tools/32-positive-manifold-visualiser/`

**What it teaches.** Six fictional tests draw on overlapping pools of
underlying processes, with no shared general ability built into the
model at all. At the default overlap, fitting a single general factor
to the resulting correlations gives loadings around 0.65, a largest
residual of about 0.015, and a factor that carries about 40% of the
variance, purely from accidental overlap between the pools each test
draws on.

**Before students start.** The six tests' overlap model is fixed; only the slider changes how
much of the shared pool each test draws on.

**What students do.**
1. Predict whether a well-fitting general factor requires a real
   general ability behind it.
2. Move the overlap slider to its default and read the loadings,
   largest residual and variance carried.
3. Take the slider down to a low overlap and compare the fit again.
4. Compare how closely the factor fits at low overlap against how much
   variance it actually carries.

**What to look for.** At the lowest overlap tried, the single factor
fits about as closely as it ever does, residual around 0.023, yet it
now carries only about 6% of the variance. A close-fitting factor and
a strong factor are different things: fit describes how well a
one-factor model reproduces the correlations, and the variance carried
describes how much of the six tests it actually accounts for.

**Debrief.** The factor fit almost as well at a low overlap as at a
high one. Does that mean the tests share almost as much in common
either way?

**Common misconception / caution.** A well-fitting general factor is
not evidence that a real general ability caused the correlations,
since this model contains no general ability at all: correlation here
comes purely from tests drawing on overlapping pools of unrelated
processes by chance.

**Use in class / timing.** Comparing the fit and the variance carried
at two settings of the slider takes about 5 minutes.
