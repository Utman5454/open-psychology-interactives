# Teaching notes - The Self Through Different Lenses (Simplified)

`simplified/modules/social-critical-psychology/tools/05-self-through-different-lenses/`

**What it teaches.** With five frameworks applied to the same nine
statements, eight end up central to at least one of them. The ninth,
a fee rise and a cut in hours in the same month, is central to none
and reachable only indirectly, as a change in a relationship, by one
framework.

**Before students start.** Nothing to prepare. Coverage tiles for
whichever framework a student picks appear as soon as it is selected.

**What students do.**
1. Predict what will mostly differ between the five frameworks.
2. Choose a framework and read its coverage tiles and its coding
   against all nine statements.
3. Once all five have been tried, reveal the full grid and the
   synthesis sentence.

**What to look for.** Eight of the nine statements are central to at
least one framework somewhere on the board. The fee-and-hours
statement is central to none, and only the relational account reaches
it at all, and only indirectly.

**Debrief.** What would it take for a framework here to treat a change
in circumstances as central rather than as background?

**Common misconception / caution.** The relational account treating
"which is the real me" as the wrong question is not the same as
saying there is no real Nadia. It declines the question rather than
answering it in either direction.

**Use in class / timing.** Working through all five frameworks takes
about 6 to 7 minutes.
