What this edition is

Each activity takes roughly three to seven minutes. You predict something, change something, see what follows, and read a short account of what it did and did not show. There is one idea to take away rather than five.

The mechanism is never the thing that was simplified. A simulation stays a simulation and a manipulable model stays manipulable; what was cut is the number of things being taught at once. Every activity links to its full-length version, which runs longer and asks more.

Nothing here needs an account, a plugin or a network connection. Nothing you do in an activity is saved or sent anywhere.
