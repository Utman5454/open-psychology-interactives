Identity can become more or less salient

Maya did not acquire or lose any group memberships. What changed was the comparison that mattered — and with it, which norms, roles and possibilities became psychologically relevant.

Key idea: Self-categorisation is context-sensitive. When a group identity becomes salient, it can shape which norms, roles and expectations feel relevant — including what we feel confident, entitled or expected to do.
