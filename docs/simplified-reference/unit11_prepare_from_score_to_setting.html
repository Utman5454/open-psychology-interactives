Measurement and explanation are different jobs

A score can identify a pattern in workers. To locate causes, we need evidence about the conditions around them and what happens when those conditions change.

Key idea: Measuring exhaustion in a worker does not mean the cause is inside the worker. A person-level outcome can be explained by job demands, resources and organisational design. The intervention should follow the evidence about where the problem is located.
