# Teaching notes - Personality Disorder Continuum

`modules/personality-individual-differences/tools/55-personality-disorder-continuum/`

**What it teaches.** Six dimensions, trait extremity, rigidity,
cross-situational persistence, subjective distress, interpersonal
impact and functional impairment, are set independently for a
fictional profile, and moving a threshold on a population of 800
simulated profiles shows how much a definition matters. At the
default threshold, requiring only unusual traits classes about 29.5%
of the population above the line; requiring unusual traits together
with genuine functional difficulty drops that to about 1.8%, from the
identical population and the identical threshold value.

**Before students start.** None needed.

**What students do.**
1. Predict whether raising a diagnostic threshold changes anything
   about the fictional people underneath it.
2. Load the "unusual, and working" preset and the "same traits, much
   more difficulty" preset and compare their extremity and functioning
   scores.
3. Switch between the two threshold rules at the same threshold value
   and watch the population share change sharply.
4. Move the threshold itself and watch the same two rules diverge
   further, or converge, as it moves.

**What to look for.** The two presets share an identical extremity
score of 85 but differ enormously in functioning, from about 21 to
about 78, because distress is deliberately excluded from the
functioning composite and rigidity, persistence and impairment can
vary independently of how unusual the underlying traits are. Nothing
about either profile's six numbers changes when the threshold or the
rule changes; only which side of the line each profile falls on does.

**Debrief.** Moving the threshold from 60 to 80 dramatically shrinks
the population counted as above the line. Did the underlying variation
in the population change?

**Common misconception / caution.** Unusual traits alone are not
sufficient for anything resembling a disorder in this model, and the
population share swinging with the threshold does not mean the
underlying difficulties are imaginary or that the categories drawn
from them are worthless; the threshold is a conventional decision, but
the variation and the difficulty it is drawn across are real.

**Use in class / timing.** Comparing the two presets and the two
rules at one threshold takes about 10 minutes. Exploring the full
threshold range, allow 20 to 25 minutes.
