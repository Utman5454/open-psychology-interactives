# Teaching notes - Factorial ANOVA Interaction Detective

`modules/research-methods/tools/11-factorial-anova-interaction-detective/`

**What it teaches.** An interaction is a difference of differences
across four cell means, and it is symmetric regardless of which factor
sits on the horizontal axis. Averaging into marginal means can make
large, real, opposite effects cancel into a main effect of exactly
zero. The tool deliberately reports no F, no p and no effect size,
since four means with no sample size behind them cannot support a real
test statistic.

**Before students start.** Nothing here is about causation. The
factorial structure alone never licenses a causal claim, whatever the
pattern looks like.

**What students do.**
1. Predict whether the pinned four-mean pattern shows a main effect of
   feedback, of task, or an interaction.
2. Edit the four cell means directly, or load a named pattern.
3. Toggle the vertical axis between zoomed and full scale, and watch
   the same pattern look dramatic or trivial.
4. Add uncertainty bars to the cell means and judge whether the
   pattern still holds up.
5. Try removing the interaction, or swapping which factor sits on the
   horizontal axis.
6. Complete the closing sentence describing the pattern in words.

**What to look for.** The "Hidden" pattern crosses, and still carries
a genuine ten-point main effect of task. A student who concludes "the
lines cross, so there is no main effect" from a crossover pattern
alone is wrong here specifically because of this preset.

**Debrief.** Feedback changed scores by twenty points in both
conditions and its main effect came out at zero; how, and what does
that tell you about what a main effect actually summarises? Write the
interaction as a subtraction one way round, then the other; why are
the two equal?

**Common misconception / caution.** "The lines cross, so there are no
main effects" is directly refuted by the Hidden pattern, which crosses
and still carries a real main effect. Nothing here is about causation;
whether a factor was manipulated or merely measured is a design
decision the plot can never reveal.

**Use in class / timing.** Showing one pattern takes about 10 minutes.
Working through several patterns with the challenge and debrief takes
about 35 minutes.
