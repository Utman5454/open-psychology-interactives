# Teaching notes - Reflexivity and Alternative Theme Builder

`modules/research-methods/tools/07-reflexivity-alternative-theme-builder/`

**What it teaches.** A research question and a theoretical lens
jointly determine which extracts become central and which recede, and
jointly determine which claims the resulting account can support. Some
question-lens pairings cohere better than others, which is a separate,
assessable standard from any one analyst's skill.

**Before students start.** Changing the question or the lens never
adds or removes anything from the six extracts, only what the account
built from them is entitled to say.

**What students do.**
1. Predict what changing a theoretical lens actually does to an
   analysis.
2. Choose a research question and a lens, and read the resulting
   account.
3. Save that account, then change the question or lens and compare
   the two side by side.
4. Try enough combinations to see both a strong pairing and a strained
   one.
5. Hold a reading to account by judging five fixed claims against your
   chosen lens.

**What to look for.** Two of the nine pairings are rated strained, and
two of the five accountability claims fail no matter which lens is
applied. One claim genuinely does flip between lenses, but for a
stated, principled reason rather than because any reading goes.

**Debrief.** Nothing was added to or removed from the six extracts by
any control; what changed, then? One claim changes verdict when the
lens changes. Is that relativism, or is there a principled reason for
the difference?

**Common misconception / caution.** "So it is all interpretation,
anything goes" does not survive the accountability check: two of nine
pairings are strained and two of five claims fail under every reading,
which is external evidence of a standard. Choosing a lens does not
license its claims automatically; even the critical lens fails a claim
that oversteps what it can support.

**Use in class / timing.** Showing one pairing takes about 12 minutes.
Comparing several with the accountability check and debrief takes
about 45 minutes.
