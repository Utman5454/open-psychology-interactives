# Teaching notes - Stroop Interference Laboratory

`modules/cognitive/tools/06-stroop-interference-lab/`

**What it teaches.** Interference and facilitation are two separate effects
measured against a neutral baseline. Naming an ink colour is slowed by a
conflicting word and helped by a matching one. Subtracting congruent from
incongruent collapses both effects into one number and hides which of them
actually moved.

**Before students start.** Say that the browser timings are illustrative
rather than a measurement of anyone, and that the neutral condition is what
makes the rest of the activity readable.

**What students do.**
1. Predict where a neutral block will sit relative to congruent and
   incongruent.
2. Run six practice trials, then a scored block, or load the worked example
   instead.
3. Read interference and facilitation off the results panel, both measured
   against neutral.
4. Work through the transfer challenge on four changes to the task.

**What to look for.** In the worked example, incongruent trials run 78ms
slower than neutral and congruent trials run 24ms faster, so neutral sits
between the two rather than beside either one. Watch for students reporting
only the incongruent-minus-congruent gap. That single number hides two
effects moving in different directions.

**Debrief.** What can you say with the neutral condition on the chart that
you could not say without it? Interference is usually larger than
facilitation. What does that asymmetry rule out for a simple "the word gets
read automatically" account?

**Common misconception / caution.** Students often reduce the Stroop effect
to "incongruent minus congruent," which throws away the very comparison the
neutral condition makes possible. Twelve trials per condition is also
nowhere near enough for a stable difference score, and no reaction time on
this page is a Stroop score for the person who produced it.

**Use in class / timing.** The worked example alone runs the whole idea in
5 minutes. A full session with a live block, the transfer challenge and
debrief takes 25 to 30 minutes.
