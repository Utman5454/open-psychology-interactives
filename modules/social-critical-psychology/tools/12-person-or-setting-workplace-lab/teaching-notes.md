# Teaching notes - Person or Setting? Workplace Explanation Laboratory

`modules/social-critical-psychology/tools/12-person-or-setting-workplace-lab/`

**What it teaches.** A correlation of 0.61 between exhaustion and
performance across one team's fourteen staff is a real, orderly
finding about how those fourteen differ from each other. It cannot be
evidence for or against a rota, a queue or a target that is the same
for all fourteen, because a constant cannot appear inside a
within-team correlation at all.

**Before students start.** Read the sorting question aloud. The rota,
the queue and the target apply to the whole team regardless of where
any one person falls on the correlation.

**What students do.**
1. Predict what the 0.61 correlation, alongside three exits among the
   most exhausted staff, actually establishes.
2. Work through two rounds of evidence, four individual-level items
   then seven more spanning relational and structural levels,
   committing an explanation after each round.
3. Spend a ten-unit budget on six interventions worth twenty units
   between them and model twelve months, then judge five claims
   against what the evidence actually supports.

**What to look for.** The rota, the queue and the target are constant
across all fourteen staff, so none of them can appear inside a
correlation computed across those same fourteen people. Spending the
full budget on the two individual and relational interventions reaches
a different modelled outcome than spending it on the two structural
ones, and neither choice is free of a trade-off.

**Debrief.** Why does a correlation being real and orderly not tell
you whether it counts for or against a constant that applies to
everyone on the team?

**Common misconception / caution.** A between-person correlation and a
between-team comparison are both real, and neither cancels the other:
the setting sets the level everybody in it sits at, and individual
differences set who sits where within it. Redesigning a rota will not
close a documented skill gap, and two teams matched at recruitment are
not matched now.

**Use in class / timing.** The first round of evidence alone makes the
point in about 8 minutes. Both experiments with the closing challenge
run to about 40.
