# Teaching notes - Measuring Prejudice: What Does the Instrument Capture?

`modules/social-critical-psychology/tools/11-measuring-prejudice-instrument-lab/`

**What it teaches.** Five instruments sit at three different levels,
individual, dyadic and institutional, and are not interchangeable
readings of one hidden trait. Two respondents 0.02 seconds apart on a
latency score shortlist 0 and 3 of 4 candidates respectively, and a
rule that predicts shortlisting from the score beats guessing "even"
by only 57% to 43%.

**Before students start.** Read the badge aloud: Vantry, its regions
and all 200 respondents are entirely simulated.

**What students do.**
1. Predict how well a sorting-task score will predict one person's
   shortlist.
2. See three respondents' latency scores in turn, predict each one's
   shortlist band, or say the score cannot tell you, then reveal the
   true shortlist against the full scatter of 200.
3. Open five instruments one at a time and judge what each directly
   observes, what is inferred from it, and what it cannot support.
4. Judge five statements against what the evidence here actually
   licenses.

**What to look for.** Self-report, the latency task and the
behavioural-choice task are three separate individual-level
instruments, not three stages toward one hidden number. The informant
report sits at a dyadic level and the twelve-year outcomes figure at
an institutional one, and no instrument at one level settles a claim
made at another.

**Debrief.** What does the twelve-year gap in institutional outcomes
license a claim about, and what does it not license a claim about?

**Common misconception / caution.** The latency task is not a more
honest version of self-report. Calling it "the real attitude,
underneath" promotes one instrument rhetorically rather than making a
measurement claim. Using any single score to select, train, reassure
or accuse one person is a category error that no significance test
repairs.

**Use in class / timing.** The first experiment alone takes about 8
minutes. Both experiments with the closing challenge extend the
session to about 40.
