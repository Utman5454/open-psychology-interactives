# Teaching notes - The Self Through Different Lenses

`modules/social-critical-psychology/tools/05-self-through-different-lenses/`

**What it teaches.** Seven frameworks for the self ask different
questions of the same nine statements, so different statements count
as central evidence under each. None of them competes to find Nadia's
real self. One statement, a fee rise and a cut in hours in the same
month, has no concept behind it in five of the seven frameworks and
can only be reached indirectly, as a change in a future self or in a
relationship, by the other two.

**Before students start.** Read aloud that there is no correct lens
here and no hidden fact about Nadia to find, before opening the board.

**What students do.**
1. Predict what will mostly differ between the seven frameworks
   applied to the same nine statements.
2. Choose a framework, watch the evidence board tag each statement
   central, usable indirectly, or no concept for it, then match one of
   four candidate accounts to that framework's own premises.
3. Apply at least four frameworks to unlock the synthesis, then judge
   five claims against whichever framework they actually follow from.

**What to look for.** No framework a student applies ever treats the
fee-and-hours statement as central. Two frameworks, possible selves
and the relational account, can reach it indirectly; the other five
have no concept for it at all, and a summary built only from those
five would never record it happened.

**Debrief.** What would an eighth framework need room for, given that
Nadia is not exhausted by any of the seven on offer?

**Common misconception / caution.** Picking the wrong account in the
matching task is not a wrong account of Nadia; it is a good account
written from different premises. "Identity is relational" is also not
the same claim as "there is no stable self," and the closing challenge
is built specifically to catch a student who slides from one to the
other.

**Use in class / timing.** Two contrasting lenses take about 10
minutes. Apply four contrasting lenses with the synthesis and
challenge, and the session runs to about 35.
