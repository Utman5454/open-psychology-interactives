# Teaching notes - Network Disconnection Mapper

`modules/neuropsychology/tools/03-network-disconnection-mapper/`

**What it teaches.** A task can fail while every region in the network
is intact, because a pathway between two intact regions has been cut.
The tool also computes, live, every other single region or pathway
whose damage alone would reproduce the same task-by-task profile, so a
symptom constrains where a failure sits without identifying it. Regions
and pathways here are functional roles for a diagram, not anatomy.

**Before students start.** Read the "this diagram is a toy" panel
aloud: five boxes and six lines, no atlas, no positions that mean
anything anatomically.

**What students do.**
1. Predict where damage would sit to explain a case with impaired
   repetition, worst for unfamiliar words, alongside normal
   comprehension and naming.
2. Load presets that damage a region or cut a pathway, or toggle
   regions and pathways freely, and read the outcome for four tasks.
3. Tick every single failure that would reproduce a given profile in
   the closing challenge.

**What to look for.** Cutting the pathway between hearing and word
sounds impairs repetition with every region intact. Damaging the
word-sound store, damaging the speech-output region, and cutting the
pathway between them all produce the identical four-task profile, and
the tool names this tie automatically rather than asserting it.
Toggling the direct picture-to-word pathway alone changes nothing on
any of the four tasks, a negative result worth pausing on.

**Debrief.** Ask what a deficit on its own can rule in or out about a
single region, given that three different single failures here produce
one indistinguishable profile.

**Common misconception / caution.** Students sometimes name preset 3's
pattern as transcortical sensory aphasia. The tool deliberately names
no syndrome: these categories are contested and fit real people poorly,
and naming one here would teach a habit the activity is built to
break. Localisation and network accounts are not presented as rivals
either: a region still does something, and what it does depends on
what it connects to.

**Use in class / timing.** The pathway-cut preset alone makes the point
in about 6 minutes. The full activity with the challenge runs to about
25 minutes.
