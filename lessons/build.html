        How a lesson works

        Students need only the link. It opens a page that walks them through your steps in order, with each activity embedded and your instructions beside it. Their answers stay in their browser; at the end they can download or copy them to bring to class or paste into a submission.

        Nothing is recorded. No one, including this site, can see who opened a lesson or what they wrote. If you want answers, ask students to hand them in.

        The link is the lesson. Anyone with it can open it, and changing the lesson makes a new link. Keep the lesson file if you want to edit it again later on another computer.

        Embedding. Paste the link into Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, Teams or an email as you would any address.
